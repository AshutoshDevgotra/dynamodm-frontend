# Instagram Auto DM Pricing in India

Auto DM tools can feel expensive when billed in dollars. For a creator just starting out, the right plan is one that is **affordable, honest, and grows with you**.

## What drives auto DM pricing?

- **DMs per month:** the more messages, the higher the tier.
- **Automation rules:** how many keyword triggers you can run at once.
- **Leads:** how many contacts the tool stores.
- **Extras:** PDF attachments, AI features, analytics, custom branding.

## How to pick a plan

1. Start free and test one reel.
2. Move to Starter when you hit the 10-DM limit.
3. Upgrade to Pro when you run many automations or send PDFs.
