# Instagram Auto DM for Ecommerce and Shopify Stores

Shoppers comment "price", "link" and "size?" under your reels. Auto DM turns those comments into product page visits.

## Flows to set up

- **SHOP:** sends the collection link
- **PRICE:** sends price and a buy link
- **SIZE:** sends the size guide
- **OFFER:** sends a discount code

## Connect a keyword to a product

If a reel features one product, connect a keyword to that Shopify page or marketplace listing so viewers land exactly where they can buy.

## Capture leads

Each request becomes a lead you can follow up with during sales or restocks.

## Tips

- Put the keyword on screen
- Mention price range or offer in the reel
- Keep DMs short with one link
- Use follow-first for new product drops to grow your audience
