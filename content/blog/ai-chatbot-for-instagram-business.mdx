# AI Chatbot for Instagram Business

Keyword triggers handle simple requests. But customers also ask real questions: "Is this in stock?", "What is the return policy?", "Which size should I pick?" An **AI context bot** can answer these.

## How it works

1. You add brand context: products, prices, policies, FAQs, offers and tone.
2. A customer comments or messages your page.
3. AI understands the intent.
4. It replies using only the context you provided.

## What it is good for

- Product questions
- Pricing and offers
- Delivery and returns
- Store timings
- Lead qualification

## Keep control

You decide what information the bot has. Add clear, accurate content and review conversations regularly. Escalate complex or sensitive issues to a human.

## Tips for better answers

- Write FAQs in plain language
- Keep prices and offers updated
- Add your brand voice guidelines
- Test with real questions before going live
