# Best Comment Trigger Keywords for Instagram Auto DM

Your trigger word is the bridge between the reel and the DM. Choose well and more people will comment correctly.

## Rules for a good keyword

- **Short:** one word, 3 to 6 letters
- **Easy to type** on a phone
- **Relevant** to the resource
- **Unique** to that reel
- **Hard to misspell**

## Keyword examples

| Niche | Keyword | Resource |
|---|---|---|
| General | LINK | Any link |
| Finance | SIP | Investing sheet |
| Fitness | PLAN | Workout plan |
| Creators | PRESET | Editing presets |
| Fashion | SHOP | Outfit links |
| Education | NOTES | Study notes |
| Business | PRICE | Price list |
| Travel | TRIP | Itinerary |

## Mistakes to avoid

- Long phrases with spaces
- Common words people use in normal comments
- Reusing the same keyword for different resources
- Forgetting to say the keyword out loud and on screen

## Make it obvious

Say it in the reel, show it as large text, and repeat it in the caption.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should keywords be in capital letters?

Capitals in your instructions help people notice them. Matching is usually not case-sensitive, but check your tool's settings.

### Can I use Hindi or Hinglish keywords?

Yes, but keep them easy to type.
