# Instagram Auto DM for Coaches and Course Creators

Your audience asks the same questions: "What is the fee?", "Where do I register?", "Send details." Auto DM answers them instantly.

## Flows that work

### Free guide to discovery call

1. Reel teaches one tip
2. "Comment GUIDE" delivers a PDF
3. The DM invites them to a free call

### Webinar registration

"Comment WEBINAR" sends the registration link and reminder details.

### Course waitlist

"Comment JOIN" sends a form link and adds the person to your leads.

## Why it fits coaches

- Reach people while they are interested
- Qualify with a short question in the DM
- Build a list you can nurture
- Save hours of repeat replies

## Tips

- Offer real value before asking for a sale
- Keep DMs conversational
- Link to one clear next step
- Add follow-first to grow your audience
