# Link in Bio vs Auto DM

Both help you share links. They solve different problems.

## Link in bio

**Pros:** always available, good for a general hub, shows all your offers.
**Cons:** viewers must leave the reel, find your profile, and choose among many links.

## Auto DM

**Pros:** delivers the exact link to the person who asked, captures a lead, and can ask for a follow first.
**Cons:** needs a clear keyword call to action and a connected tool.

## Use both

- **Auto DM** for the specific resource in a reel
- **Link in bio** for your overall storefront
