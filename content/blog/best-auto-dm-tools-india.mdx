# Best Auto DM Tools in India: What to Look For

Searching for the **best auto DM tool in India**? Features matter, but so do price, support, and whether the tool understands how Indian creators work. Use this checklist.

## 1. Pricing in rupees

Dollar pricing adds currency conversion and card friction. A tool with INR plans is easier to budget. Orange Flow starts at **₹99/month**, with a free plan of 10 DMs.

## 2. Comment-to-DM triggers

The core job: someone comments a keyword, the tool sends a DM. Check that you can set multiple keywords, write a public reply, and attach links or PDFs.

## 3. Follow-first option

A follow-first step asks people to follow your account before they get the resource. It turns every giveaway into a growth loop. Learn more in [follow-first auto DM](/blog/follow-first-auto-dm-instagram).

## 4. Lead capture

You should see who triggered the automation, not just a count. Leads help you follow up and measure which reels bring real interest.

## 5. Policy-aware sending

Good tools pace messages and include opt-out safeguards. Read our guide on [whether auto DM is safe](/blog/is-instagram-auto-dm-safe).

## 6. Creator-economy extras

Link-in-bio storefronts, affiliate link delivery, and brand collaboration opportunities make a tool more than a one-trick automation.

## 7. Local support

Support that understands Indian creators, platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and Myntra, and INR payments saves time.
