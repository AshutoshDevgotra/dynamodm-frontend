# The "Comment LINK" Strategy

You have seen it everywhere: *"Comment GUIDE and I'll send it to you."* It works because it is a simple exchange of value, and automation makes it scalable.

## The strategy in four steps

### 1. Create a resource worth asking for

A PDF checklist, template pack, tool list, recipe, price sheet, or a link to a product. It should solve a specific problem from your reel.

### 2. Make the reel point to it

Teach something useful, then add the call to action: **"Comment LINK and I'll send you the resource."** Put the keyword on screen as text.

### 3. Set a trigger word

In [Orange Flow](https://orangeflow.in), create an automation with your keyword. When a viewer comments it, the tool sends the DM automatically.

### 4. Add follow-first

Before the viewer receives the link, the DM asks them to follow your account. They get the resource, you get a follower. That is the real value of the loop.

## Why it performs

- Comments rise, which signals engagement
- Followers grow with each resource
- Leads are captured automatically
- You save hours of manual replies

## Examples by niche

- **Finance:** "Comment SIP for my beginner investing sheet"
- **Fitness:** "Comment PLAN for the 7-day workout"
- **Edit:** "Comment PRESET for my free pack"
- **Fashion:** "Comment SHOP for the outfit links"

## Keep it ethical

Deliver exactly what you promise, keep messages relevant, and respect opt-outs. Read about [safe automation](/blog/is-instagram-auto-dm-safe).

## Frequently asked questions

### What should my resource be?

Anything specific and useful: a checklist, template, guide, tool list, or product link that matches the reel.

### How many keywords can I use?

Use one clear keyword per reel so people know exactly what to comment.
