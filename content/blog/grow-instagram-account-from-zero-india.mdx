# How to Grow an Instagram Account From Zero in India

Starting from zero feels slow, but a clear plan beats random posting.

## Week 1: Foundation

- Pick one niche and one audience
- Write a bio that states who you help and how
- Switch to a Creator or Business account
- Plan 10 reel ideas

## Week 2: Publish and learn

- Post 3 to 4 reels with strong hooks
- Reply to every comment
- Note which topics get saves and comments

## Week 3: Add a resource loop

- Create one free resource (checklist, template, list)
- Set up a keyword in [Orange Flow](https://orangeflow.in)
- Say "Comment GUIDE" in your reels
- Turn on follow-first so requests become followers

## Week 4: Double down

- Make more of what worked
- Collaborate with 2 to 3 creators in your niche
- Review leads and follow up with value

## Daily habits

- 10 minutes engaging with niche accounts
- One story with a poll or question
- Quick review of comments and DMs

## Be patient and consistent

Early growth is mostly learning. The creators who win keep showing up and improve each reel.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long to reach 1,000 followers?

It varies widely by niche and consistency. Focus on steady posting and resource-driven reels.

### Do I need paid ads?

No. Organic reels with a strong call to action can work well.
