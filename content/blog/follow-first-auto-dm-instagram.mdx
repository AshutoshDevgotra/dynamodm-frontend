# Follow-First Auto DM: Turn Comments Into Followers

A normal auto DM sends the link immediately. A **follow-first auto DM** adds one smart step: it asks the person to follow your account before they receive the resource.

## How the flow works

1. You post a reel with a valuable resource.
2. You say: "Comment **GUIDE** and I'll send it."
3. Someone comments GUIDE.
4. Orange Flow sends a friendly DM: "Follow us first, then tap here to get your guide."
5. After the follow step, the link is delivered.

The viewer gets what they wanted, and you gain a follower who already likes your content.

## Why it works

- **Fair exchange:** you give something useful, they give a follow.
- **Warm audience:** these followers actively asked for your content.
- **Compounding growth:** every reel with a resource can add followers.
- **Higher engagement:** comments push your reel, followers see your future posts.

## Best practices

- Offer something truly valuable, not a vague teaser.
- Keep the DM short and friendly.
- Explain why they should follow ("I share a new template every week").
- Deliver what you promised, every time.

## Example scripts

> "Comment SIP and I'll send you my free SIP starter sheet."

> "Comment EDIT and I'll DM my 10 video editing presets."
