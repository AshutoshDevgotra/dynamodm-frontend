# Instagram Reel Ideas That Get Comments

Comments grow when you **ask clearly and offer something**. These formats work in most niches.

## 1. The quick tutorial

Teach one step in 20 seconds. CTA: "Comment STEPS for the full guide."

## 2. The "tools I use" list

Show 5 tools. CTA: "Comment TOOLS and I'll send the list."

## 3. Before and after

Show a result, then offer the process. CTA: "Comment HOW."

## 4. Template reveal

Give away a template. CTA: "Comment TEMPLATE."

## 5. Mistakes to avoid

"3 mistakes beginners make." CTA: "Comment AVOID for my checklist."

## 6. Product walkthrough

Show a product in use. CTA: "Comment SHOP for the link."

## 7. Myth vs fact

Bust a myth in your niche, then link to more.

## 8. Weekly resource drop

Same format each week builds habit and anticipation.

## 9. Behind the scenes

Show your process and offer the tools behind it.

## 10. Giveaway

Ask people to comment a keyword to enter. See [giveaway automation](/blog/instagram-giveaway-automation).

### How often should I ask for comments?

Use a clear call to action on most value reels, but vary formats so it feels natural.

Engagement such as comments, saves and shares is a useful signal, though no one controls the algorithm.
