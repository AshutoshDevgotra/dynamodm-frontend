# Instagram DM Automation for Small Businesses in India

For many Indian brands, Instagram is the shop window. Customers comment "price?", "link?", "available?" all day. Answering each one by hand is slow and expensive.

## What can you automate?

- Product link requests
- Price and availability questions
- Store location and timings
- Offers and coupon codes
- Lead capture for enquiries

## Two ways to automate

### Keyword triggers

Customers comment **PRICE** or **LINK** and get a DM with the right details.

### AI with brand context

When someone messages your page, AI replies using the products, prices, policies, FAQs, and tone you provide.

## Benefits

- Faster replies, even at night
- Fewer missed sales
- Consistent answers
- A lead list from every conversation
- No need for a large support team

## A starter setup

1. Add 3 keywords for your best products
2. Add an FAQ for delivery, returns and payment
3. Track leads weekly
4. Upgrade as volume grows
