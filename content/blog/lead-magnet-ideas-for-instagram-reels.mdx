# 25 Lead Magnet Ideas for Instagram Reels

A great call to action needs a great offer. Here are ideas you can create in an afternoon.

## Documents and templates

1. Beginner checklist PDF
2. Weekly planner template
3. Content calendar sheet
4. Budget or expense tracker
5. Caption swipe file
6. Hashtag and keyword list
7. Resume or portfolio template

## Guides and lists

8. "10 tools I use" list
9. Step-by-step mini guide
10. Recipe or meal-plan PDF
11. Travel itinerary
12. Book or course recommendations
13. Gift guide with product links
14. Outfit links from your reel

## Digital assets

15. Video editing presets
16. Canva template pack
17. Notion template
18. Thumbnail or reel cover pack

## Offers and access

19. Discount code
20. Free consultation booking link
21. Webinar registration
22. Community or channel invite
23. Early access to a product
24. Free sample or trial link
25. Giveaway entry link

## Quality over quantity

One useful resource beats ten vague ones. Make it specific, short, and easy to use.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should a lead magnet be?

Short and useful. A one-page checklist often performs better than a long ebook.

### Do I need a website?

No. You can share a PDF or a Drive link directly in the DM.
