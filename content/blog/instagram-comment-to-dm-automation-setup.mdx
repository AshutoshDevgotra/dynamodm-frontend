# How to Set Up Instagram Comment-to-DM Automation

You can be live in about five minutes. Here is the process using [Orange Flow](https://orangeflow.in).

## Before you start

- An Instagram **Business or Creator** account
- A resource to share (link, PDF, or product page)
- A reel or post ready to publish

## Step 1: Connect Instagram

Sign up at Orange Flow and connect your Instagram account to your workspace.

## Step 2: Create a new automation

Open **Automations** and click **New rule**. Choose the post or reel the rule applies to.

## Step 3: Pick a trigger keyword

Choose a short, easy-to-type word such as **LINK**, **GUIDE**, or **SIP**. Avoid words people might type by accident. See our [keyword ideas](/blog/best-comment-trigger-keywords-instagram).

## Step 4: Write the DM

Keep it friendly and clear:

> "Hey! Thanks for commenting. Here's your free guide: [link]"

Attach your link or PDF.

## Step 5: Turn on follow-first (optional)

Ask people to follow your account before they receive the resource. This is the best way to grow followers with automation.

## Step 6: Add a public reply (optional)

A quick comment reply like "Sent! Check your DMs" tells others the process works.

## Step 7: Test it

Comment the keyword from another account and confirm the DM arrives.

## Step 8: Mention it in your reel

Say it out loud and show it as text on screen: "Comment GUIDE to get it."

## Troubleshooting

- No DM? Confirm the account type and connection.
- Wrong post? Check which post the rule is attached to.
- Low comments? Make the call to action clearer.
