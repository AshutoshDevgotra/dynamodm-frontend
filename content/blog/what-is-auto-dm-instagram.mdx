# What Is Auto DM on Instagram?

**Auto DM** (automatic direct message) is a tool that sends a DM to someone the moment they take an action on your Instagram, most commonly when they **comment a keyword** on your reel or post. Instead of replying to hundreds of people by hand, you set the rule once and the tool delivers the link, PDF, or answer for you.

## How auto DM works

1. You publish a reel and say, "Comment **LINK** and I'll send it to you."
2. A viewer comments the keyword.
3. The auto DM tool detects the trigger and sends your message with the resource.
4. The viewer is saved as a lead, so you know who asked.

## Why creators use auto DM

- **Save hours:** no more copy-pasting the same link into DMs.
- **Faster replies:** people get what they asked for within seconds, while interest is high.
- **More comments:** a clear "comment to get it" call-to-action lifts comment counts, which signals engagement.
- **Lead capture:** every request becomes a contact you can follow up with.
- **Follower growth:** with a follow-first step, people follow you before they receive the resource. See [how follow-first auto DM works](/blog/follow-first-auto-dm-instagram).

## What can you send with auto DM?

Free guides and PDFs, affiliate links, product pages, course or webinar links, discount codes, booking links, templates, and checklists.

## Who needs auto DM?

Content creators, coaches, course sellers, small online stores, agencies, and any brand that gets the same questions in comments again and again.
