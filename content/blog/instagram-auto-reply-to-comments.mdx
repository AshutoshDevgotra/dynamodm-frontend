# Instagram Auto Reply to Comments

When a reel gets attention, comments can pile up faster than you can answer. **Auto reply** handles the repeat ones so you can focus on real conversation.

## When to automate comment replies

- Keyword requests ("LINK", "PRICE", "DETAILS")
- Frequently asked questions
- Giveaway entries
- Thank-you replies for resource requests

## When not to automate

Complaints, sensitive topics, and thoughtful questions deserve a personal reply.

## The two-part reply

1. **Public reply** under the comment: "Sent! Check your DMs 💛"
2. **Private DM** with the link or answer

This tells other viewers the offer is real, which encourages more comments.

## Write replies that sound human

- Use simple language
- Vary the wording if the tool allows it
- Add an emoji or two, not twenty
- Match your brand voice

## Example replies

> "Done! Sent you the guide in DMs 🙌"

> "Check your inbox, your link is there!"

## Frequently asked questions

### Will auto replies hurt my reach?

Genuine, relevant replies are fine. Avoid spammy repetition and keep replies useful.

### Can I reply to every comment automatically?

It is better to target keyword comments and answer others yourself.
