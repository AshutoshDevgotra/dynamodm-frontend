# How to Send Links on Instagram DM Automatically

Instagram captions do not make links clickable, so creators say "link in bio" or "DM me." That is slow. **Auto DM** sends the exact link to the exact person who asked.

## What links can you send?

- Affiliate links (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra and more)
- Shopify or website product pages
- Lead magnet PDFs and Google Drive links
- Webinar, course or booking links
- Discount codes

## Step-by-step

1. Connect your Business or Creator account to [Orange Flow](https://orangeflow.in).
2. Create a rule with a keyword like **LINK** or **SHOP**.
3. Paste the destination link or attach a PDF.
4. Optionally enable follow-first.
5. Publish your reel and mention the keyword.

## One keyword per product

If a reel features a specific product, connect a keyword to that product link so viewers land exactly where they want to be.

## Tips for higher click-through

- Say what the link contains
- Keep the DM under a few lines
- Put the link early in the message
- Track which reels send the most traffic

## Compared with link in bio

Link in bio is a general doorway. Auto DM is a personal delivery. Read [link in bio vs auto DM](/blog/link-in-bio-vs-auto-dm).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I send affiliate links?

Yes, from programs you participate in. Availability and commission terms are set by each platform.

### Can I send different links per reel?

Yes. Create a separate rule per reel or product.
