# Instagram Lead Generation for Creators

A follower is nice. A **lead** is someone who raised their hand and asked for something. Leads buy, book, subscribe, and refer.

## What is a lead on Instagram?

Someone who comments your keyword, requests your resource, or messages you with interest.

## A simple lead system

1. **Attract** with a valuable reel.
2. **Offer** a related free resource.
3. **Capture** the request with a comment trigger.
4. **Deliver** by auto DM.
5. **Follow up** with value and an offer.

## Lead magnets that convert

Checklists, mini-courses, templates, price lists, and discount codes. See [lead magnet ideas for reels](/blog/lead-magnet-ideas-for-instagram-reels).

## What to do after capturing a lead

- Send a helpful follow-up
- Invite them to a newsletter or community
- Offer a paid product or service at the right moment

## Measure what matters

Track triggers per reel, DMs delivered, leads captured, and conversions. Improve the reels that perform best.
