# Instagram Affiliate Marketing in India

Affiliate marketing pays you a commission when people buy through your link. The challenge on Instagram: **links are hard to share.** Auto DM fixes that.

## The workflow

1. Show a product in your reel (outfit, gadget, kitchen item, book).
2. Say: "Comment **SHOP** and I'll send the link."
3. The viewer comments.
4. Orange Flow sends the product link in DM and saves the lead.

## Where to find affiliate programs

Marketplaces and brands in India offer programs, including destinations such as Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and Myntra. Availability and commission terms are set by each platform, so read the rules.

## Tips

- Recommend only what you genuinely like
- Disclose affiliate links as required by platform and advertising rules
- Use one keyword per product or collection
- Link to the specific product, not the homepage
- Review which reels drive the most triggers
