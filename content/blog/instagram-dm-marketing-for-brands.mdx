# Instagram DM Marketing for Brands

DMs are where interest turns into action. Brands that respond quickly and helpfully win more customers.

## Three types of DM marketing

### 1. Comment-to-DM

Viewers comment a keyword and receive product details, offers or links.

### 2. Inbound conversations

Customers message your page. AI or your team answers with the right context.

### 3. Creator-driven DMs

Creators share your product with their audience through auto DM links. House of Orange helps connect brands with creators.

## Build a simple funnel

1. Content or creator reel
2. Keyword call to action
3. Auto DM with the product link
4. Follow-up with offer or reminder
5. Purchase

## Best practices

- Keep messages short and helpful
- Use brand voice
- Provide one clear next step
- Respect consent and opt-outs
- Review conversations weekly

## Frequently asked questions

### Is DM marketing only for big brands?

No. Small brands often benefit most because it saves time and catches leads they would miss.

### Can creators promote my brand with auto DM?

Yes, creators can share your product link by keyword when they feature it in a reel.
