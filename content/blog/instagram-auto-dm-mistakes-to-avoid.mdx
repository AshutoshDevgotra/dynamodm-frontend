# 10 Instagram Auto DM Mistakes to Avoid

## 1. Unclear keyword
Pick a short, easy word and show it on screen.

## 2. Weak resource
If the resource is vague, people stop trusting your offers. Make it useful.

## 3. Long, pushy DMs
Keep it short: greeting, link, one line of value.

## 4. Broken or wrong links
Test every automation before posting.

## 5. Overpromising
Deliver exactly what you advertise.

## 6. Skipping follow-first
You miss the growth benefit. Turn it on for resource reels.

## 7. Using one keyword for everything
Use unique keywords per reel so you know what worked.

## 8. No follow-up
A lead is the start. Follow up with value and a next step.

## 9. Ignoring analytics
Check triggers, DMs and leads weekly.

## 10. Forgetting policy and consent
Message only people who engaged, space messages out, and provide opt-outs. See [is auto DM safe](/blog/is-instagram-auto-dm-safe).

## Quick checklist before you post

- Keyword spoken and shown
- Test comment sent
- Link opens correctly
- DM reads well on mobile
- Follow-first decided

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the biggest mistake?

Not testing. Always comment from a second account to confirm the DM arrives.

### How often should I review automations?

Weekly is a good habit, and after each viral reel.
