# Instagram Giveaway Automation

Giveaways spark comments and follows. Automation keeps entries organised and instant.

## Giveaway flow

1. Post a reel announcing the prize
2. Ask people to comment **WIN**
3. The tool sends a DM with entry rules and a follow request
4. Entries are logged as leads
5. You pick a winner fairly and announce it

## Plan the rules clearly

- Prize and value
- Eligible countries or regions
- Entry deadline
- How the winner is chosen
- How the winner is contacted

## Follow Instagram's promotion rules

Instagram has guidelines for promotions, and you must follow local law as well. State that the promotion is not sponsored by Instagram.

## Make it worthwhile

A relevant prize attracts your target audience, not just prize hunters. Pick something your followers would genuinely want.

## After the giveaway

Thank everyone, share a resource with all participants, and keep the community engaged.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is follow-first allowed in giveaways?

Asking people to follow is a common practice, but always check current Instagram promotion guidelines and local rules.

### How do I pick a winner?

Use a transparent method, such as a random draw from your logged entries.
